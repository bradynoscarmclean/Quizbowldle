      How to play

      • 1Every day has a new category and an answer from its 50 most-asked tossup answers on QB Reader.
      • 2Pick a difficulty. The hardest clue comes first, and they get easier as you go.
      • 3You start with 1000 points. Each new clue costs 100.
      • 4A wrong answer costs 250. Three wrong answers and you're out.
      • 5On the 10th clue you get one final attempt. Prompts ("be more specific") are free.

      Clue difficulty is based on how early it appears in the original tossup and whether it's before the power mark (*).
