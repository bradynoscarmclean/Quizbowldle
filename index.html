      How to play

      • Every day there's a new category and an answer pulled from its 50 most-asked tossup answers on QB Reader.
      • Pick a difficulty. You'll see the hardest clue first; clues get easier as you go.
      • You start with 1000 points. Each new clue costs 100.
      • Buzz whenever you know it. A wrong buzz moves you to the next clue.
      • On the 10th clue you get one final attempt.
      • If you're asked to be more specific (a prompt), you can guess again for free.

      Clues are scored by how early they appear in the original tossup and whether they're before the power mark (*).
